Privacy Policy

Cassette · last updated 2026-05-21

Cassette is a small, family-built app for saving short video memories into private scrapbooks. This page describes what data the app collects, where it lives, and how to ask for it to be deleted. It is intentionally short because the app is intentionally small.

What we collect

  • Account info: the email address and password you use to sign in. Passwords are hashed and never stored in plain text.
  • Your videos: the short video clips you upload to your scrapbooks.
  • Scrapbook metadata: scrapbook names, captions you write, the order of clips, timestamps, and other text you enter inside the app.
  • Basic technical logs: connection events and error logs needed to keep the app running. These are kept short-term for debugging and are not used for marketing or profiling.

What we do not collect

  • No advertising identifiers, no ad networks, no third-party trackers.
  • No analytics SDKs that profile your behaviour.
  • No contact lists, no photo library scans beyond the videos you explicitly pick.
  • No location data.

Where your data lives

  • Accounts and scrapbook metadata are stored in Supabase, which provides the database and authentication service.
  • Video files are stored in Cloudflare R2, an object storage service. Videos are private to your account.

Both providers operate under their own published privacy policies (linked above). We do not sell, rent, or share your data with any other party.

Who can see your scrapbooks

By default, scrapbooks are private to your account. If you choose to share a scrapbook (for example, by generating a share link or inviting another account), only the people you share with can see it. We do not make scrapbook contents public or browsable.

Children's privacy

Cassette is designed for adults who want to save family memories, including memories of their own children. The app does not have public profiles, no chat features, and no discovery surface. If you believe a child has created an account without parental consent, please email us and we will remove the account and its contents.

Deleting your data

To delete your account, your scrapbooks, and all uploaded videos, email cstewch@gmail.com from the email address tied to your account. We will confirm and complete deletion within 30 days. Backups containing the deleted data are overwritten in the normal course of operations within 90 days.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy in a meaningful way, the "last updated" date above will change, and signed-in users will see a notice the next time they open the app.

Contact

Questions, deletion requests, or anything else: cstewch@gmail.com.
